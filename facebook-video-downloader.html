How to copy a Facebook video link

  1. Open the video or Reel in the Facebook app.
  2. Tap Share under the post.
  3. Tap Copy link. On some videos this option is in the three-dot menu instead.

In a browser, open the video on its own and copy the address from the address bar. The link should point to the video or Reel, not to a whole page or profile.

Save it with AllVid, step by step

  2. Choose from the formats and sizes AllVid finds.
  3. Wait for the file to save. ⚡ sizes start straight away; others may take a short "getting ready" step.
  4. Go to My files to watch, share or remove it.

Using the AllVid Android app? Tap Share on the Facebook post, choose to share to another app, and select AllVid.

Formats and sizes

Facebook often provides a standard and a higher quality version of each video. AllVid lists what is available, usually as MP4 video in sizes such as 360p or 720p and sometimes higher, plus MP3 or M4A audio where available. Longer videos and higher sizes make bigger files.

What works and what does not

  • Works: public videos and public Reels, including your own posts shared publicly.
  • Does not work: videos shared only with friends, posts in private groups, anything that needs a login, expired stories, live videos while they are live, deleted videos, and links to pages or profiles.

For your own videos that are not public, Facebook's "Download your information" tool in its settings can export a copy of your content.

Tips

  • Pick 720p for watching on a phone, and the highest size offered for keepsakes.
  • Download long videos on Wi-Fi with the browser tab open, or use the Android app so the download keeps going with the screen off.
  • Save audio only for talks, sermons or speeches you recorded or have permission to keep.
  • Back up important family videos to a computer or cloud storage as well.

Keeping family and event videos safe

Facebook is often where families share birthdays, weddings and holidays, and where local groups and businesses post event videos. If you filmed and posted those videos yourself, saving a copy means you still have them if an account is closed or a post is removed. When relatives or friends posted the video, ask them before saving it, or ask them to send you the original file, which will be better quality than the version Facebook stores. For videos that include children, be thoughtful about where copies end up.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Facebook account to use AllVid?

No. AllVid works with public video links and never asks you to log in to Facebook.

Why does my Facebook link not work?

The video may be shared with friends only, posted in a private group, deleted, or still live. Check that it is public and that you copied the video's own link.

Can I save videos from a Facebook page I follow?

Only if you have the page owner's permission or the video is offered for download. Following a page does not give you the right to copy its videos.

Does AllVid keep a copy of the video?

No. The file is saved to your device, and your list of downloads stays on your device too.
