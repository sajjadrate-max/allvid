About AllVid

Last updated: October 25, 2026

AllVid is a free web tool that helps people save videos and audio they are allowed to keep, such as their own uploads or videos the owner has permitted others to save, from popular sites to their phone for offline viewing.

How it works

You paste a link or share it to AllVid. AllVid looks up the files the site makes available for that post and lets you choose a size. The file is then saved on your own device. AllVid does not keep a copy of any video on its servers.

Responsible use

Only save content you own or have permission to save. Respect the creators and the rules of each site. See our Terms of Use and Copyright & Removal page.
