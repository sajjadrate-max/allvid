How to copy a Pinterest link

  1. Open the video Pin in the Pinterest app.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Tap Copy link. Pinterest often gives you a short link that starts with pin.it.

In a browser, open the Pin and copy the address from the address bar. Make sure it is a video Pin; image-only Pins have no video to save, and links to boards or profiles will not work.

Save it with AllVid, step by step

  1. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download, or open AllVid and paste it there.
  2. Pick a format and size.
  3. Wait for the file to save. ⚡ sizes start immediately; others may need a short "getting ready" step.
  4. Open My files to watch, share or remove the video.

In the AllVid Android app, you can also tap Share on a Pin and send it straight to AllVid.

Formats and sizes

AllVid lists the versions Pinterest makes available, usually as MP4 video in one or more sizes, and MP3 or M4A audio where available. Many video Pins are short, so files are often small.

What works and what does not

  • Works: public video Pins, including short pin.it links.
  • Does not work: image-only Pins, Pins on secret boards, anything that needs a login, deleted Pins, links to boards or profiles, and Pins that only link out to a video hosted on another website.

If a Pin links to a video on another site, the video belongs to that site and its creator; visit the original source instead.

Tips

  • Saving a recipe or craft video you made yourself is a great way to keep a backup of your work.
  • For someone else's tutorial, saving the Pin to a board in Pinterest is usually the simplest, permission-friendly option.
  • Choose a smaller size if you just want to follow along in the kitchen; there is no need for the largest file.
  • Delete videos you have finished with from My files to keep storage free.

Why save your own video Pins?

If you run a blog, a small shop or a creative hobby, your video Pins are part of your portfolio. Saving copies means you can reuse them on your website or other platforms you run, keep an offline archive, and recover them if a Pin is removed or your account changes. The saved file matches the version Pinterest stores, so keep your original recordings for editing. For other people's ideas, Pinterest's own save-to-board feature is the best way to keep track of them while giving the creator credit.

Frequently asked questions

Do pin.it short links work?

Yes. Short pin.it links and full Pinterest Pin links both work, as long as the Pin contains a video and is public.

Why does AllVid say there is no video?

The Pin may be an image, an idea Pin without video, or a Pin that only links to a video on another website. Check that the Pin plays a video inside Pinterest.

Do I need a Pinterest account?

No. AllVid works with public Pins and never asks for your Pinterest login.

Can I save any video Pin?

Only your own, or ones the creator allows you to keep. Many Pins are tutorials made by small creators, so ask first or save the Pin to a board instead.
