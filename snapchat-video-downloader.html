How to copy a Snapchat link

  1. Open the Spotlight video or public Story in Snapchat.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu or the Share button. On some screens you can press and hold the video to see these options.
  3. Tap Copy link.

On the Snapchat website, open the Spotlight or public Story and copy the address from the address bar. Private snaps and chats do not have shareable links.

  4. Open My files to watch, share or remove it.

Formats and sizes

Spotlight videos are short and vertical. AllVid lists the versions available, usually as MP4 video, and MP3 or M4A audio where available. Because Snapchat videos are short, files are usually small and quick to save.

What works and what does not

  • Works: public Spotlight videos and public Stories while they are still available.
  • Does not work: private snaps and chats, friends-only Stories, Stories that have expired, anything that needs a login, deleted content, and links to profiles.

Snapchat's privacy model matters: people often share snaps expecting them to disappear. AllVid does not get around those settings, and you should never save someone's private content without their consent.

Tips

  • Save public Stories soon, because they expire and cannot be saved afterwards.
  • For your own snaps, Snapchat's Memories feature is another way to keep a copy.
  • Rename saved files with the date, as Snapchat clips can be hard to tell apart.
  • Back up favourite Spotlights to a computer or cloud storage.

Why creators save their Spotlight videos

Spotlight lets anyone reach a wide audience, and many creators post the same short videos on several platforms. If you recorded and edited a Spotlight inside Snapchat, the posted version may be your only copy. Saving it lets you keep an offline archive, rewatch it without data, or reuse it on other channels you run. Before reusing a video that includes friends, check they are happy with it appearing somewhere new. Respecting what people expected when they shared is part of using Snapchat well.

Frequently asked questions

Can AllVid save private snaps?

No. Private snaps, chats and friends-only Stories cannot be saved with AllVid, and saving other people's private content without consent is not okay.

Why did my Story link stop working?

Stories expire after a short time. Once a Story has expired or been deleted, it can no longer be saved.

Do I need to sign in to Snapchat?

No. AllVid only works with public links and never asks for your Snapchat login.

Can I save someone else's Spotlight video?

Only with the creator's permission. Spotlight videos belong to the people who made them, so ask first or share the link.
