Copyright & Removal

Last updated: October 25, 2026

We respect the rights of creators. AllVid does not host any video or audio files; it only helps users save files that the original sites make available.

If you own the rights to content and believe AllVid is being used to infringe them, please contact us with:

  • your name and how to reach you;
  • the address (URL) of the original content;
  • a description of the work and of the rights you hold;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized.

We will review every request and, where appropriate, block the content from being processed by AllVid.

Send requests through our Contact page.
