Copyright and removal requests

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Creators put time, skill and often money into what they make. AllVid is built to help people keep their own videos and content they are allowed to save, not to take work from the people who made it. This page explains how rights holders can ask us to stop processing their content and what happens next.

Our respect for creators

Our Terms of Use allow AllVid to be used only for content a person owns or has permission to save. We ask every user to respect creators, and we publish guidance such as copyright basics for everyday users to help people understand what that means in practice. When a rights holder tells us that their content is being misused, we take it seriously.

We do not host content

It is important to understand how AllVid works. AllVid does not host, store, index or publish videos or audio. There is no library or catalog of content on our site. When a user submits a link, the file is fetched from the original site, prepared if needed, and delivered to that user's own device. The user's list of downloads stays on their device, and we do not keep copies of files.

This means we cannot remove a video from the internet, and we cannot delete a file from someone's phone. If your content is posted somewhere without your permission, the most effective step is to report it to the site where it is posted, such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Snapchat or Pinterest. Each of these platforms has its own copyright reporting process.

What we can do is stop AllVid from processing specific links to your content.

How to submit a removal request

Send requests by email to sajjadrate@gmail.com, or open a new request on our public request page at github.com/sajjadrate-max/allvid/issues (you can also reach it from our contact page) and include:

  1. The work. A short description of the content you own, such as "my cooking video posted on 3 March".
  2. The exact links. The full URL of each post you want AllVid to stop processing.
  3. Who you are. Your name, or the name of the company or creator you represent, and your relationship to the work.
  4. A good-faith statement. A sentence stating that you believe in good faith that use of the content through AllVid is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
  5. An accuracy statement. A sentence stating that the information in your request is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  6. A way to verify. For example, a link to a post on your official account that mentions the request, or a public profile we can match to your name.

Because the request page is public, please do not post your home address, phone number or other private details. If we need more information to verify a request, we will ask on the request itself.

What we do

When we receive a complete request and can reasonably verify it, we block AllVid from processing the listed URLs. After that, anyone who submits one of those links to AllVid will not be able to prepare or save it through our service. We will reply on your request to confirm what was done. We aim to handle copyright requests ahead of other requests, but we cannot promise a specific response time.

If a request is incomplete or we cannot verify it, we will explain what is missing so you can update it.

Mistakes and counter-notices

Sometimes a link is blocked by mistake, for example if a request listed the wrong URL or came from someone who does not actually own the content. If you believe a link to your own content, or content you have permission to use, has been blocked in error, please open a request that includes:

  • the blocked link or links;
  • why you believe the block is a mistake, such as proof that you are the creator or that you have a license; and
  • a statement that the information you give is accurate.

We will review it and, where appropriate, ask the original requester for their view. If the block was clearly a mistake, we will lift it. Please do not file false requests or false counter-notices; they waste everyone's time and may have legal consequences.

Repeat misuse

AllVid has no accounts, but we still act on clear patterns of abuse. If we see that the service is being used repeatedly to process content that rights holders have asked us to protect, or to copy content in bulk, we may block the links involved, limit or block the traffic responsible, and take other reasonable steps to protect creators and the service.

Questions

If you are unsure whether to send a request, or have a question about this process, please get in touch through our contact page.
