How to copy an Instagram link

  1. Open the Reel or video post in the Instagram app.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the paper plane) below the video, or tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap Copy link.

On the Instagram website, open the post and copy the address from your browser's address bar. Links to a single Reel or post work best; links to a whole profile do not.

Formats and sizes

For most Reels and video posts AllVid offers MP4 video in the sizes Instagram makes available, which can include 720p or higher depending on the upload, plus MP3 or M4A audio where available. Instagram compresses uploads, so the saved file matches Instagram's version rather than the original from your camera.

What works and what does not

  • Works: public Reels and public video posts.
  • Does not work: posts from private accounts, anything that needs you to log in, Close Friends content, stories that have expired, live broadcasts, deleted posts, and links to profiles.

AllVid does not log in to Instagram or get around privacy settings. For a full copy of your own account, Instagram's "Download your information" tool in its settings is another option.

Tips

  • If a post contains several items, make sure you copied the link to the post that includes the video.
  • Choose 720p for a good balance of quality and size on a phone; go higher for keepsakes if offered.
  • Save only the audio when you want your own voiceover or a talk you recorded.
  • Rename important files with a date so they are easy to find later.

Good reasons to save your own Reels

Creators and small businesses often reuse their best Reels: in a portfolio, on a website, in an ad they run themselves, or on another platform. If you edited a Reel inside Instagram, the posted version may be the only finished copy you have. Saving it gives you an offline archive that does not depend on your account staying active. If you collaborate with other creators on a Reel, agree together on how each of you may reuse it, and remember that music added from Instagram's library may not be licensed for use elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to log in to Instagram to use AllVid?

No. AllVid never asks for your Instagram username or password. It only works with public posts.

Can I save someone else's Reel?

Only if the creator has given you permission. Reels are protected by copyright, so ask first, or share the link instead so the creator gets the credit.

Why can I not save an Instagram story?

Stories disappear after a short time, and many need a login to view. Expired stories cannot be saved. If it is your own story, Instagram's archive feature may still have it.

Is the quality the same as the original?

The saved file is the same quality Instagram stores, which is usually compressed compared with your original recording. Keep your camera originals for the best quality.
