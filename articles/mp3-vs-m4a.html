MP3 vs M4A audio explained

Published October 25, 2026 · 3 min read

When you only need the sound from a video, such as a talk, a lesson, or a voice memo you recorded, saving audio alone makes a much smaller file. AllVid usually offers two audio formats: MP3 and M4A. Here is what the difference is and how to choose.

What these formats are

Both MP3 and M4A are ways of storing compressed audio. Compression removes parts of the sound that most people cannot hear, so the file is far smaller than a raw recording while still sounding good.

  • MP3 has been around since the 1990s. It is the format most people think of when they hear "music file", and almost every device made in the last twenty-plus years can play it.
  • M4A is an audio file that usually contains sound encoded with AAC (Advanced Audio Coding), a newer method designed to improve on MP3. The name comes from the MPEG-4 container it uses. It is the standard audio format behind a lot of modern streaming and is widely supported on phones.

Sound quality

At the same file size, AAC audio in an M4A file generally sounds a little better than MP3, especially at lower bitrates. Bitrate is the amount of data used per second of audio, measured in kilobits per second (kbps). At higher bitrates, such as 192 kbps or more, most people find it very hard to tell the two apart on ordinary headphones or a phone speaker.

One important point: converting audio never adds quality. Most videos online already store their sound as AAC. Saving that as M4A can often keep the sound as it is, while saving it as MP3 means converting it to a different format, which can lose a tiny bit of detail. For spoken word you will rarely notice, but for music it is one reason to prefer M4A when your devices support it.

File size

File size depends mainly on bitrate and length rather than format. As a rough guide, one minute of audio at 128 kbps is about 1 MB, and at 256 kbps about 2 MB. Because AAC is more efficient, an M4A file can sound as good as an MP3 while being somewhat smaller. Either way, audio is tiny compared with video: an hour-long talk as audio might be 60 MB, while the same talk as 720p video could be several hundred megabytes.

Compatibility

This is where MP3 still shines. It plays on practically everything: old MP3 players, car stereos, smart speakers, older computers and almost any app. M4A plays on all modern iPhones and Android phones, current computers and most music apps, but some older car stereos and basic devices do not recognise it.

If you are not sure where a file will end up, MP3 is the safe choice.

Which one should you pick?

  • Choose M4A when you will listen on a modern phone, tablet or computer and want the best quality for the size, or when keeping the original sound matters.
  • Choose MP3 when you need the file to play on older devices, a car stereo, or equipment you do not control, or when someone specifically asks for an MP3.

For most people listening on their own phone, either works well. M4A has a slight edge on quality and size; MP3 has the edge on playing everywhere.

Why audio may take a moment

When you choose MP3, AllVid usually has to convert the sound, so you may see a short "getting ready" step before the download starts. M4A can sometimes be saved more directly. For long recordings, the conversion step takes a bit longer, so it helps to keep the page or app open until it finishes. In the Android app, once the download itself begins, your phone's download manager takes over.

Good uses for audio-only saves

  • Lectures, sermons, or interviews you recorded or have permission to keep
  • Your own podcast episodes or voice recordings that you posted as videos
  • Music that the artist offers for free download or under a license that allows it
  • Language-learning material shared for personal study by its creator

As with video, only save audio you own or are allowed to keep. Music in particular is usually protected by copyright, and a song being on a video site does not mean you may download it. Our article on saving videos responsibly explains more.
