Posted a clip on X, formerly Twitter, and want to keep a copy? AllVid saves the video from your own posts, and from public posts whose creator has allowed you to keep it, to your phone as a regular file. It is free, there is no account, and you never need to share your X login.

How to copy a link on X

  1. Open the post that contains the video in the X app.
  2. Tap the Share icon under the post.
  3. Tap Copy link.

In a browser, open the post on its own and copy the address from the address bar. Links on both x.com and twitter.com point to the same post. Make sure you copy the link to the post with the video, not to a profile.

Save it with AllVid, step by step

  1. Paste the link into the box above and tap Download, or open AllVid and paste it there.
  2. Choose a format and size.
  3. Wait for the file to save. ⚡ sizes start right away; others may need a short "getting ready" step.
  4. Open My files to watch, share or remove it.

In the AllVid Android app, you can tap Share on a post, choose to share via another app, and pick AllVid.

Formats and sizes

X usually stores videos in several sizes. AllVid lists the ones available as MP4 video, for example a small size for quick viewing and 720p or higher where the upload allows, and MP3 or M4A audio where available. Short animated GIFs on X are actually stored as small videos, so they may also save as MP4.

What works and what does not

  • Works: public posts that contain a video, including your own.
  • Does not work: posts from protected accounts, posts that need a login or are age-restricted, deleted posts, live broadcasts and Spaces, and links to profiles or lists.

If a post quotes another post with a video, open the original post and copy its link instead.

Tips

  • For news-style clips you want to reference, sharing the link usually gives better context and credit than a copy.
  • Choose a smaller size on mobile data; pick a higher one on Wi-Fi for keepsakes.
  • Save audio only for your own voice notes or talks posted as video.
  • If a download fails, try again in a minute or pick a ⚡ size.

Why save videos from your own posts?

X is fast-moving, and older posts can be hard to find again. If you post clips of your own work, talks, gaming moments or events you filmed, saving them lets you keep an offline archive, reuse them on other platforms you run, or include them in a portfolio. X also offers a way to request an archive of your account data in its settings, which can be useful for a complete backup. For single clips, saving from the post with AllVid is often quicker. Keep important files organised and backed up somewhere other than your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Does AllVid work with twitter.com links?

Yes. Links from x.com and twitter.com both work, as long as they point to a single public post with a video.

Do I need to sign in to X?

No. AllVid never asks for your X username or password, and it only works with public posts.

Can I save videos posted by other people?

Only when the creator has given you permission or the video is offered under a license that allows it. Otherwise, share the link instead.

Why does the link say it cannot be saved?

The account may be protected, the post may be deleted or age-restricted, or the video is a live broadcast. Check the post is public and try again.
