Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page explains how AllVid uses cookies and similar technologies on allvid.org and in the AllVid Android app, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Websites also use similar technologies, such as local storage and IndexedDB, to remember information on your device. In this policy we call all of these "cookies" for simplicity.

Cookies AllVid sets itself

AllVid does not use cookies to track you, build a profile about you, or show you personalized content. We only store what the app needs to work, and it stays on your device:

  • Settings: your language, theme (light, dark or automatic), preferred download quality and how many downloads run at once.
  • Your files list: the videos and audio you saved with AllVid, the time you saved them, and downloads that are still in progress, so they survive closing the page.
  • Small conveniences: for example, which tab was open, so a refresh brings you back to the same place.

This information is never sent to us. It is kept in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB (or the app's own storage on Android). These items are strictly necessary for the features you use, so they do not require consent.

Website statistics (Google Analytics)

On the website (not inside the AllVid Android app) we use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they come from, so we can improve AllVid. Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_* and collects information like pages viewed, device and browser type, and an approximate location. We do not send Google your name or the files you save. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Cookies used by our hosting provider

Our website is hosted by Vercel. To deliver pages quickly and protect the service from abuse, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type. AllVid does not use this information to identify you.

Advertising cookies (website only)

We may show advertising on allvid.org to keep AllVid free. Ads are not shown inside the AllVid Android app by Google advertising services.

If ads are shown on the website, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the consent message's settings.

How to control cookies

You are always in control:

  • Delete your AllVid data: open Settings in AllVid and choose "Delete all files", or clear the site data for allvid.org in your browser settings.
  • Block cookies: most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their privacy settings. In Chrome on Android, open the menu, then Settings, then Site settings, then Cookies.
  • Android app: clearing the app's storage in your phone's Settings, under Apps, removes everything AllVid has stored.

If you block all storage, AllVid may not be able to remember your settings or keep your list of files.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
