Contact AllVid

Last updated: October 25, 2026

AllVid does not use email for support. Instead, every message goes through our public request page on GitHub, where you can open a new request and follow replies. Choose the topic below that fits, and include the details listed so we can help quickly.

Open the request page

Please note: requests on that page are public. Do not include passwords, phone numbers, home addresses or other private details. A link to a public post is fine; a link to a private one is not needed.

Report a problem

Something broke, a download stopped, or a size is missing? Tell us:

  • Which site the link came from (for example TikTok or YouTube)
  • Whether you used the website or the Android app
  • Your phone model and browser
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • Any error message, ideally as a screenshot

Copyright / removal request

If you own content and do not want AllVid to process it, include:

  • The exact link or links to the content
  • Your name and your connection to the work
  • A short statement that you own the rights or act for the owner
  • How we can confirm the request, such as a post on your official account

Read the full process on our Copyright page.

Privacy question

Want to know what data is handled, or how to clear it? Include:

  • Your question in plain words
  • Whether it is about the website or the Android app

Most answers are already in our Privacy Policy. Your downloads and settings live only on your device.

Suggestions

Ideas make AllVid better. When you share one, include:

  • What you would like to do
  • Why it would help you
  • Any app or site that already does it well

We read every suggestion, even if we cannot build all of them.

What to expect after you write

AllVid is run by a small team, so we cannot promise a reply within a set time. We look at new requests regularly and give priority to copyright requests and problems that affect many people. If we need more detail, we will ask on your request, so it helps to check back now and then. Once an issue is fixed we will usually say so on the same request.

Before you contact us

Many questions have a quick answer already. Before opening a request, it is worth trying these steps:

  • Read the FAQ, which covers stopped downloads, iPhone saving, file sizes and more.
  • Try the link again after a few minutes. Sites sometimes slow down or change how they serve video.
  • Make sure the post is public. Private posts and content behind a login cannot be saved.
  • Check that your phone has enough free storage. Our guide on freeing up phone space can help.
  • Look through existing requests on the request page in case someone already reported the same issue. Adding a short comment there is often faster than opening a new one.

Thank you for helping make AllVid more reliable for everyone.
