Frequently asked questions

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Quick answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, please visit our contact page.

Which links work, and which do not?

Work: public posts from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), Snapchat and Pinterest.

Do not work on this website: YouTube links; private or deleted posts; posts that need you to log in; live streams; stories that have expired; and links to profiles or whole pages instead of a single video. If a link does not work, AllVid shows a short message saying why.

Is AllVid free?

Yes. AllVid is free to use, with no paid plan, trial period or hidden charge. We may show ads in the future to cover running costs; if that happens, our Privacy Policy will be updated first.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or email address. Open allvid.org or the Android app, paste a link and choose a size.

What can I save with AllVid?

Only content you own or have permission to keep: your own uploads, videos the creator has said you may download, and content shared under a license that allows it. Saving other people's work without permission can break copyright law and the site's rules, and it is not allowed by our Terms of Use. Our guide on saving videos responsibly explains the basics in plain words. It is not legal advice.

Where do my files go?

Files are saved on your own device. In a phone browser they usually land in your Downloads folder, or wherever your browser saves files. In the Android app they are saved by the phone's download manager, normally into Downloads. Your list of saved files appears under My files in AllVid. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Why does My files show a time next to each download?

Many videos from the same account have similar names, so My files shows the date and time each file was downloaded. That makes it easy to spot what you just saved and to tidy up older files. From My files you can watch, share or remove any file.

Why do some videos say "getting ready"?

Some sites do not offer a ready-to-save file in every size or format. In those cases AllVid has to prepare one first, for example by joining separate video and sound tracks or converting to MP3. That takes a little time, especially for long or high-quality videos. Keep the page or app open until it finishes.

What do the ⚡ sizes mean?

A ⚡ next to a size means a ready-made file already exists for it, so the download starts immediately with no preparation step. If you are in a hurry, choose a ⚡ size.

Which audio formats can I choose?

Where audio is available you can usually choose MP3 or M4A. MP3 plays almost everywhere. M4A often sounds as good or better at a smaller file size and is well supported on modern phones. Read MP3 vs M4A explained for more.

Which video size should I pick?

360p is small and saves data, 720p is a good balance for phone screens, and 1080p looks sharpest on large screens but takes the most space. Our guide 360p vs 720p vs 1080p goes into detail. You can set a preferred quality in Settings.

Why use the Android app instead of the website?

The Android app hands each download to your phone's own download manager. That means downloads keep going even if you close the app or the screen turns off, which is handy for big files or slow connections. You can also share links straight to AllVid from other apps. Get it from the "Get the AllVid Android app" button on the home page.

Do I need to keep AllVid open while downloading?

On the website, yes: keep the tab open and your screen on until downloads finish, because browsers may pause pages running in the background. In the Android app, no: once a download has started, the phone's download manager carries on by itself.

Does AllVid work on iPhone?

Yes, in the Safari browser. There is no iPhone app. After a download finishes, Safari may show the file in its Downloads list; to keep it in the Files app or save a video to Photos, open the file and use the Share button, then choose "Save to Files" or "Save Video".

My download stopped. What should I do?

First check your internet connection and free storage, then try again; a retry often works. On the website, make sure the tab stayed open and the screen did not lock. If a site is busy, wait a few minutes or pick a different size, especially a ⚡ one. If it keeps failing, please report it through the contact page with the site name and what you saw.

My phone says storage is full. Any tips?

Remove files you no longer need from My files, choose smaller sizes such as 360p or audio only, and clear out old apps and duplicate photos. See how to free up space on your phone. In Settings you can also delete all saved files at once.

Can I use AllVid in my language?

Yes. AllVid is available in many languages, and you can choose yours at any time in Settings. You can also choose a light, dark or automatic theme there.

Does AllVid keep a copy of my videos or a list of what I saved?

No. Files are delivered to your device, and your My files list and settings are stored only in your browser or the app on your phone. We do not keep copies of files or a history of your downloads. The link you paste is sent to our server so it can find the file, and our hosting provider keeps basic technical logs for a limited time. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything AllVid has stored?

To remove saved files, open Settings in AllVid and use the option to delete all files, or remove them one at a time in My files. To clear settings and the downloads list on the website, clear site data for allvid.org in your browser. In the Android app, clear the app's storage in your phone's Settings, or uninstall it.

Why does a link say it cannot be saved?

The most common reasons are that the post is private, has been deleted, needs a login to view, or the owner has restricted it. AllVid does not get around those settings. Sometimes a site is simply busy or has changed how it delivers video, so trying again later can help. Make sure you copied the full link using the app's Copy link button; see using the Share button on Android.

The Android app is an APK file. Is that safe?

Installing an APK is a normal Android feature, but you should only install apps from sources you trust. Get the AllVid app only from the "Get the AllVid Android app" button on allvid.org; if another site offers an "AllVid" app or asks you to pay for it, it is not from us. Our guide to installing apps from outside the Play Store walks through the steps and what to check.

Why does my saved video look less sharp than the original?

Social sites compress videos when they are uploaded, so a saved copy can only be as good as the version the site stores, not the original from the camera. Choosing a higher size helps, but it cannot add detail that is not there. Learn more in how video compression works and resolution vs bitrate.

How can I send a saved video to family without it getting blurry?

Chat apps usually compress videos again when you send them. Turning on an HD option, sending the file as a document, or sharing a cloud link keeps much more quality. Our guide to sharing videos on WhatsApp explains each option. Please only pass on videos that are yours or that the creator allows you to share.
