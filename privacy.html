Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information AllVid ("we") uses when you visit allvid.org or use the AllVid Android app, and why.

What we do not collect

AllVid has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information.

Links you submit

When you paste or share a link, it is sent to our server so we can find the files the site offers for it. Our server may pass the link to third-party services that prepare or provide the file (for example video conversion services). We do not store the links you submit beyond normal, short-lived server logs.

Data stored on your device

Your downloads, settings (language, theme, preferred quality) and the list of your files are stored only in your own browser or app storage on your device. You can delete them at any time from Settings → Delete all files, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Server logs and hosting

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request in server logs, to keep the service secure and working. These logs are kept for a limited time.

Cookies and advertising

AllVid itself does not use cookies for tracking. If we show advertising in the future, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

Children

AllVid is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see our Contact page.
